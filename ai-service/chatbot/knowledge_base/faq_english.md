# Internet Banking FAQ (English)

## Account Opening
Register on the "Register" page with your name, email, and phone. Then upload KYC documents — NID front/back and a selfie. Admin approves within 24-48 hours.

## Fund Transfer
Go to Dashboard → Transfer. 4 steps:
1. Select source account
2. Enter beneficiary account number
3. Enter amount
4. Verify OTP

Minimum: ৳1 | Per transaction: ৳2,00,000 | Daily: ৳5,00,000.

## OTP
OTP is sent to your registered phone/email. Valid for 5 minutes. 3 failed attempts = 30-min account lock.

## KYC
"Know Your Customer" — submit NID, address, and photo. Once approved, account becomes ACTIVE. Transfer requires KYC.

## Bill Payment
Go to Bills → "Add Bill". Pay utility bills, mobile recharge, etc.

## Card Block
Go to Cards → select card → "Block". You can unblock anytime.

## Loans
Go to Loans → "Apply for Loan". AI gives a credit score. Score 60+ = higher approval chance.

## Complaints
Go to Support → create a new ticket with subject, description, and priority. Response within 24-48 hours.
